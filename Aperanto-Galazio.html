Aperanto Galazio

You're eligible for a Genius discount at Aperanto Galazio! To save at this property, all you have to do is sign in. Just 150 metres from the sandy beach of Aegiali in Amorgos, the Cycladic-style Aperanto galazio offers air-conditioned rooms with free Wi-Fi and balcony overlooking the Aegean Sea. It features a bar with sea views and is only 100 metres from the port.

A TV, fridge and hairdryer are included in all rooms at the Aperanto Galazio. Each comes with a safety deposit box.

Breakfast is served on the spacious terrace overlooking the sea. Traditional cafés, family taverns and shops with folk art are within a 3-minute walk.

Staff at the 24-hour front desk can offer information on villages such as Potamos, Tholaria and Lagkada. Car rental services are provided.The scenic town of Amorgos is at 15 km.

Couples particularly like the location — they rated it 9.4 for a two-person trip.
